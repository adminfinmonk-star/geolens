Hover Peek Interaction
"Hover icon → secondary panel peeks"

Hovering over any primary rail icon temporarily slides out that suite’s secondary links as an overlay without disrupting your current canvas workspace.

Click to Pin (Active State)
"Click icon → secondary stays pinned"

Clicking a suite icon locks the secondary panel into persistent mode (pushing canvas content right) and highlights the suite tile with soft mint styling.

3
Full Collapse Control
"<< Collapse → hide secondary, rail remains"

Clicking the double-chevron collapse trigger at the base of the rail cleanly docks the secondary panel away, maximizing horizontal screen real estate for deep analysis.
